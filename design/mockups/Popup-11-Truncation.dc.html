History of the Roman Empire - Wikipedia

en.wikipedia.org

Long page: summarised the first 60,000 characters

TL;DR

The Roman Empire ruled the Mediterranean world for centuries after Augustus became the first emperor in 27 BC. The western half collapsed in AD 476; the eastern half lasted until 1453.

Key points

  • Augustus ended the Republic’s civil wars and began two centuries of relative stability, the Pax Romana.
  • The empire reached its greatest extent under Trajan in AD 117.
  • The Crisis of the Third Century brought civil war, invasions and economic strain.
  • Constantine legalised Christianity and founded Constantinople.
