How do C4 plants avoid wasting energy in hot weather?
Gemini

In hot, dry weather leaves close their stomata, CO₂ inside the leaf drops, and RuBisCO starts grabbing O₂ instead. That wasteful side-reaction is photorespiration.

C4 plants split the job across two cell types:

  1. Mesophyll cells capture CO₂ with PEP carboxylase, which ignores oxygen, and store it as a four-carbon acid.
  2. Bundle-sheath cells release that CO₂ and run the Calvin cycle in a CO₂-rich pocket.

Maize, sugarcane and sorghum all use this route.
