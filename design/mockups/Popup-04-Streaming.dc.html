Photosynthesis - Wikipedia

en.wikipedia.org

TL;DR

Photosynthesis is how plants, algae and some bacteria turn light, water and carbon dioxide into sugar, releasing oxygen as a by-product. It powers almost every food chain and keeps the air supplied with oxygen.

Key points

  • Happens mainly in chloroplasts, where chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light and reflects green.
  • Runs in two stages: the light-dependent reactions split water and make ATP and NADPH; the Calvin cycle uses them to fix CO₂ into sugar.
  • RuBisCO does the fixing and is often called the most abundant protein on
