Summarising your selection

TL;DR

The selected passage explains that photosynthesis runs in two linked stages: one captures light energy, the other spends it building sugar from CO₂.

Key points

  • Runs in two stages: the light-dependent reactions split water and make ATP and NADPH; the Calvin cycle uses them to fix CO₂ into sugar.
  • The Calvin cycle is light-independent but still depends on the ATP and NADPH made in the light.
